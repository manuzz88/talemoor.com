Talemoor is a home for illustrated stories. Every tale comes to life panel by panel, carried by a narrator's voice, like leafing through a book that breathes. Adventure, mystery, legend, drama: different genres, one place.

Our publishing tool

Talemoor uses a private tool connected to the YouTube Data API to publish and manage videos on its own YouTube channel: uploads, titles, descriptions, translations, subtitles, playlists and channel artwork. The tool only accesses the Talemoor channel and never accesses data belonging to other YouTube users. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
